# GP Truck Aid | Brand Identity & Visual Development

The GP Truck Aid project included the development of a visual identity to establish a recognisable brand for the heavy-vehicle assistance and transport support concept.

Three logo concepts were developed during the branding process. These explored different visual approaches for representing the business and its connection to the heavy-vehicle industry.
