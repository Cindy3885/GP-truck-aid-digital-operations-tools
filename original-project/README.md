# Original GP Truck Aid Project

## Background

GP Truck Aid was developed as a digital business concept serving the heavy-vehicle transport and logistics industry.

The original project brought together emergency assistance services, fleet management resources, vehicle-related tools, driver safety information, mechanical maintenance guidance, industry articles and workplace health and safety resources.

It was designed to provide more than a conventional service website: the aim was to create a useful online resource hub for businesses, fleet operators and drivers.
